Privacy Policy

Ops Assistant · Last updated November 1, 2026

Ops Assistant is a private, internal tool operated by Klay Hafeez for his own franchise businesses in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a QuickBooks Online company, Ops Assistant can access that company's accounting data through Intuit's API: accounts, customers, vendors, invoices, payments, deposits, expenses, journal entries and reports. It may also access the owner's field-service systems (such as ServiceTitan, ServiceMinder and Jobber) to compare them with the books.

How it is used

The data is used only to produce reports for the owner, find bookkeeping problems, and make corrections the owner approves. It is not sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Ops Assistant runs on the owner's own computer and private accounts. Access tokens and API keys are stored in private, access-restricted files on that computer and are never published. Data may be processed by the AI assistant service the owner uses to run the tool (Anthropic's Claude) to carry out the owner's requests.

Customer information

Business records may include names and contact details of the businesses' customers. This information is handled only for the businesses' own bookkeeping and operations, in line with Canada's PIPEDA and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act.

Retention and disconnecting

The owner can disconnect a QuickBooks company at any time from QuickBooks (Apps) or by revoking access. Stored tokens for that company are then deleted. Logs of changes made are kept privately by the owner as bookkeeping records.
