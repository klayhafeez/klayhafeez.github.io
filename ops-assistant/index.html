Ops Assistant

Private internal tool

Ops Assistant is a private bookkeeping and operations tool used only by Klay Hafeez to manage his own franchise businesses in Calgary, Alberta. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

It connects to the owner's own QuickBooks Online companies to read reports and transactions and, with the owner's approval, to make bookkeeping corrections.
